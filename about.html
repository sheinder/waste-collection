About Us

Making waste collection organized, one pickup at a time

Waste Collection started with a simple goal: replace paper logs and phone calls with a system that customers and admins can both rely on. Today, we help communities request, schedule, and track waste pickups digitally.

🎯 Our Mission

Make waste collection simple and transparent for every household and business we serve.

🌱 Our Vision

Cleaner, more sustainable cities where waste is managed efficiently and responsibly.

🤝 Our Values

Reliability, transparency, and a genuine commitment to the communities we operate in.
