Waste Types

Know what you're throwing out, and where it goes

Different waste needs different handling. Here's how we categorize what we collect, so pickups are sorted correctly from the start.

Sorting starts with you

Selecting the right waste type when you request a pickup helps our team handle it correctly and keeps recyclable material out of landfill.

  • Choose a waste type when requesting a pickup
  • Keep hazardous items separate from general waste
  • Bag or bundle recyclables before collection day
